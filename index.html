How I lead

I build the system around the work.

High-quality outcomes are rarely a matter of individual design talent alone. They come from a clear product direction, the right decisions at the right time, and a team that can challenge and support each other.

  1. 01

    Make the problem clear

    Turn broad requests into a shared understanding of the user, the job to be done, the product constraint, and the decision that matters.

  2. 02

    Give design a place in the operating model

    Use discovery, readiness, critique, handoff, and impact signals as connected practices—not isolated ceremonies.

  3. 03

    Grow capability through the work

    Mentor designers, make quality criteria visible, and create reusable methods that raise the team’s confidence and craft.

  4. 04

    Keep evidence close to decisions

    Bring research, usage signals, and customer context into roadmap choices so teams learn what actually improves the product.
