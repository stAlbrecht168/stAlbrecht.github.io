Leadership

How I lead design at scale

Running UX across six agile scrum teams means design can't be reactive. I build the operating models, processes, and quality criteria that give design a consistent place in the planning cycle, from first brief through shipping and impact tracking.

  • 01

    UX Operating Model

    A 7-step model integrating SAP's PDLC, defining the UX contribution at each stage from discovery through delivery, with explicit quality gates.

  • 02

    Design Readiness Framework

    5-dimension readiness scoring per epic, reducing late scope changes by identifying design uncertainty before quarterly planning locks.

  • 03

    Team Capability Building

    Skills Registry across the CSX UX chapter, 1:1 mentoring for up to four designers, and a shared AI practice strand growing chapter-wide capability.

  • 04

    Stakeholder Alignment

    Translating design rationale into product and engineering language, making design decisions defensible through evidence, not preference.

AI Strategy

AI-first product strategy

Before AI can improve a product, you need to know what "good" looks like. I built the evaluation frameworks, interaction principles, and capability infrastructure that give the team a rigorous basis for AI design decisions.

  • 01

    Agent Output Evaluation

    6-dimension benchmark framework (Value Alignment, Output Completeness, Format Accuracy, Cognitive Load, Trust, Interaction Efficiency) applied to Joule service result guidance.

  • 02

    Trust-First Automation

    Interaction patterns for explainable automation: making agent decisions visible, reversible, and appropriately supervised across planning, lifecycle, and service workflows.

  • 03

    Skills Registry & AI Practice

    Chapter-wide capability map with AI practice strand, agentic workflow design, evaluation methodology, and trust-first pattern libraries.

Research

Research as a strategic capability

Research isn't just validation, it's the mechanism that changes what a product team believes. I use field research, contextual inquiry, and structured synthesis to surface insights that shift roadmap priorities, not just screen layouts.

  • 01

    Contextual Inquiry

    Field research with users in real operating environments, workshops, cable test bays, vehicle cabs, to surface constraints that would never appear in a usability lab.

  • 02

    Synthesis Frameworks

    Workflow analysis of 24+ applications, qualitative research synthesis, and AI benchmark scoring, turning raw data into defensible design rationale.

  • 03

    Evidence-Based Iteration

    Usability studies with SUS scoring, analytics-to-design synthesis pipelines, and UX maturity tracking, moving teams from opinion to evidence.

About

Stephan Albrecht

I'm a UX Design Lead at SAP, currently leading design across SAP Cloud ALM, a cloud operations platform used by enterprise IT teams globally. My work spans UX strategy, operating model design, team leadership, and AI experience design.

Before SAP, I spent a decade as a consulting UX lead, working across financial services (BBVA, Helaba, Citi), industrial and medtech (Bosch, Zeiss, BAUR, Heidenhain), life sciences (SAP iCTSM), and media (BBC, Vodafone, Ericsson). Each engagement added a different dimension to how I think about design at the intersection of complexity, constraint, and user trust.

My current focus is the intersection of large-scale UX strategy and AI product design, how organisations build the capability to ship AI features that are genuinely useful, trustworthy, and evaluable.

Based in Germany. Open to strategic conversations about design leadership roles.
