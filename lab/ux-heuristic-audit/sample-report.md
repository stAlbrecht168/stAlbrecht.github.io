## Prioritised findings

### 1. Help and documentation — severity 3/4 (score 1/3)
**Evidence:** No visible onboarding, tooltips, or explanation of what "points", "karma", or the ranking are. A first-time visitor cannot tell how the site works from the page itself.

**Fix:** Add a lightweight "new here?" link near the masthead that opens a one-screen explainer; keep it dismissible for returning users.

### 2. Aesthetic and minimalist design — severity 2/4 (score 2/3)
**Evidence:** Dense monochrome list with low contrast between metadata and titles; everything competes at the same visual weight.

**Fix:** Increase the type-size and contrast gap between the link title and its metadata row so the primary action (the headline) wins the scan.

### 3. Visibility of system status — severity 2/4 (score 2/3)
**Evidence:** Upvoting gives immediate feedback, but pagination ("More" at the bottom) gives no indication of position or how many pages remain.

**Fix:** Show a page indicator or infinite-scroll affordance so users know where they are in the list.

### 4. Error prevention — severity 2/4 (score 2/3)
**Evidence:** Destructive-adjacent actions (flag, hide) sit inline with no confirmation and are easy to hit by mistake on touch.

**Fix:** Add an undo toast for flag/hide rather than a hard confirm, preserving speed while catching slips.

### 5. Match between system and the real world — severity 1/4 (score 3/3)
**Evidence:** Language is plain and conventional; link labels match destinations.

**Fix:** Maintain; consider spelling out "comments" consistently rather than a bare count on some rows.
